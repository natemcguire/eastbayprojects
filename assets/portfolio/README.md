### SailScan generation prompt

Mode: built-in image generation, reference-image edit / product-mockup.

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: premium design studio portfolio feature, landscape 4:3 composition.
Primary request: Transform authentic SailScan iOS app marketing screenshots into a beautiful editorial device composition with the polish of the Droptics reference.
Input images: Image 1 is the SailScan results UI and must be used on the foreground phone; Image 2 is the SailScan camera UI for the rear phone; Image 3 is ONLY the art-direction reference for angled device realism and clean bold background, do not copy its branding or lime color.
Scene: seamless rich saturated ocean turquoise studio backdrop, subtly lit, no scenery.
Subject: two slim graphite iPhones floating at complementary slight angles, results phone large and front right, camera phone slightly smaller behind left. Balanced sculptural arrangement, mild perspective to keep screens readable, delicate realistic shadows beneath and fine edge highlights.
Screen invariants: reproduce supplied real app screen content, sail photographs, tracing lines, navy UI, ivory type, coral buttons and 12.4° metric faithfully. Crop away the marketing headline and outer navy margins from the camera source, use just the inner camera UI as its phone screen. Crop outer margins from results source. Do not invent additional UI.
Composition: entire phones visible with generous safety margin on all edges, fill approximately 80% of frame height, clean full-bleed backdrop. Professional high-end product rendering, crisp glass, restrained reflections.
Avoid: headlines, decorative text, logos outside the app, watermark, extra objects, waves, fake UI, exaggerated perspective, cropped phones.
```
