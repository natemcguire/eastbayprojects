Still Water Privacy Policy

Still Water is built to keep your pool data private. It is a local-first app: your readings, pools, and reminders live on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Still Water does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter stays on your device. If you subscribe to Still Water Pro, your data may sync across your own Apple devices using Apple's iCloud (your private iCloud database). We never see it — that sync happens directly between your devices and your iCloud account, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases of Still Water Pro are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children

Still Water does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date above.
