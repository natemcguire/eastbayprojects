Common questions

How do I log a test?
Dip a test strip, then on the Today screen tap the color that matches each pad. Tap "Log today's test" and Still Water shows exactly how much of each chemical to add for your pool's size.

How do I set my pool size?
Go to the Pool tab and enter your pool's volume in gallons. Dosing guidance is calculated from this number.

What's in Still Water Pro?
Multiple pools, your full testing history, unlimited reminders, and iCloud sync across your devices. Choose a one-time purchase or a low yearly price.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the paywall (tap any Pro feature) and choose "Restore Purchases."

Is my data private?
Yes. Everything stays on your device and your own iCloud. See our Privacy Policy.
