HOW IT WORKS

01

Agree on the work

Tell us what the campaign needs, when it needs to launch, and who will approve the work. We set out the scope, fees, schedule, and advertising budget.

02

Prepare the content

We collect the text, photographs, account access, and disclosures needed for the project. We also write website copy and produce ads when that work is included in the scope.

03

Build & review

We develop the website and advertising included in the project. Your team reviews the pages, forms, and ads and approves them before launch.

04

Launch & manage

We launch the approved work, check the website and forms, and manage the agreed updates and advertising. Reports cover spending, ad performance, website traffic, and form submissions where tracking is available.
