A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before we start.

Can you build something from scratch?

Yes. Tell us what the software should do and what your team uses today. We help define the requirements and a first release.

Will you use my existing code?

We review it first. We explain what can stay, what needs changes, and whether anything should be replaced before starting development.

Do I need to be a developer?

No. You need to explain what the app should do. We explain the technical choices and review the software with you.

How do you agree on the cost and timeline?

We review the application and the work involved, then provide a proposal. Where more investigation is needed, we agree on an initial assessment before quoting the development work.

Where will the app run?

In hosting accounts you control. We choose the setup based on the application, your existing systems, and the cost of running it.

Who owns the software? Are there ongoing costs?

You own the custom code we build for you and control the deployment accounts. Hosting, AI usage, and third-party services may have ongoing costs. Existing libraries and services retain their own licenses. Ongoing maintenance can be scoped separately.

What happens after launch?

We hand over the code and operating instructions and walk you through the setup. Your team can maintain the app, or we can agree on further development and support.
