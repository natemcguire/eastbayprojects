SEA TREK / SAUSALITO, CALIFORNIA

THE WHOLE TEAM.
ONE DAY
ON THE BAY.

Make a mixed kayak-and-electric-boat company outing easy to organize, on dates the business can profitably accommodate.

Independent opportunity study based on public sources. Proposed work, not an implemented project or a client endorsement.

GROW FROM AN EXISTING STRENGTH / SEPTEMBER 2026

THE BUSINESS / THE EXISTING OFFER

TWO WAYS TO JOIN.
ONE GROUP TO ORGANIZE.

Sea Trek identifies Galen Licht and Kiki Wykstra as co-owners and operates from Sausalito and Alameda. Its corporate program already combines guided paddles with an electric-boat option for guests who prefer to cruise. Meet the team ↗

THE OPPORTUNITY

A clear offer can help an organizer include colleagues with different activity preferences, get internal approval, and plan another outing. Test whether that produces additional bookings.

THE FIRST QUESTION

Which dates, guides, and boats can accommodate more work at a worthwhile contribution? Confirm this before improving the sales process or seeking more inquiries.

THE PROPOSED FIRST PILOT

ONE OFFER.
A FEW APPROVED DATES.

  1. Cost the real experience.

    Confirm the group mix, guide requirements, boat allocation, package inclusions, weather terms, and contribution after delivery costs.

  2. Make the proposal easy to share.

    Give the organizer an approved date, total, activity choices, meeting plan, and next step in one coherent proposal.

  3. Connect the booking handoffs.

    Bring the inquiry, staff-approved quote, deposit, participant preferences, and delivery checklist together using existing tools first.

  4. Deliver and learn.

    Seek two additional completed outings with acceptable actual contribution. Learn whether the mixed offer or planning support changed the booking decision.

A SMALL FIRST STEP

FIND THE AGREEMENT
BEFORE THE INVESTMENT.

A 3–5 business day mutual-fit review. Up to six hours from our pair and two from the company. No review fee or obligation to continue. Examine a few bookings, the calendar, and the cost of one offer.

The operations partner defines the offer and delivery rules. The engineer connects the smallest useful workflow. If the review supports a project, agree scope, price, owners, and milestones before building.

ECONOMICS / WHAT WE WOULD MEASURE

ADDITIONAL OUTINGS.
ACTUAL CONTRIBUTION.

Completed, paid work

Exclude normal bookings, canceled events, and bookings merely moved from another date or location.

Full delivery costs

Deduct qualified staffing, boat and equipment costs, package commitments, refunds, and any contribution displaced from other work.

Cost to make it work

Subtract implementation, tools, and ongoing staff effort. Future deposits and quotes are not earned profit.

Stop or simplify if the calendar is full, the current workflow already meets the need, or the available contribution cannot support the project. Use real seasonality rather than annualizing a few fall bookings.
