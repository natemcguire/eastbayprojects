THE WAY WE WORK

TWO ROLES.
ONE ACCOUNTABLE TEAM.

An engineer who gets close to the business. An operations expert who connects commercial judgment, product thinking, and the work on the ground. Together, you take a project from the first conversation to a system people actually use.

ROLE / 01

FORWARD-DEPLOYED ENGINEER

You can sit with a customer, understand a messy workflow, and turn it into working software. You care about what happens after the code ships.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Observe the work alongside an Operations Expert and help define a practical first project.
  • Build and connect applications, APIs, automations, and AI tools where they are useful.
  • Work with existing systems and real constraints: access, reliability, privacy, and the people using them.
  • Deploy, troubleshoot, document, and improve the solution with the customer’s team.

WHAT YOU BRING

Experience shipping software people depend on. Good judgment about when to build, configure, or simplify. Clear communication and a willingness to work directly with customers.

ROLE / 02

OPERATIONS EXPERT

You understand how a business makes money and gets work done. You can find the bottleneck, earn the team’s trust, and help a better process take hold.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

  • Spend time with owners and frontline teams to understand customers, costs, capacity, and daily work.
  • Turn observations into a clear problem, a useful scope, and a measurable definition of success.
  • Partner with the engineer to test solutions, coordinate delivery, and train the team.
  • Check adoption and business results, then decide what is worth improving next.

WHAT YOU BRING

Hands-on experience in operations, business ownership, product, or service delivery. You can work through numbers and processes, ask good questions, and follow a project through.
