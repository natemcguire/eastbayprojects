NORTH BAY, CALIFORNIA / FOOD MANUFACTURING

FROM CAFÉ INTEREST
TO REPEAT ORDERS.

Straus Family Creamery — Could a clearer path from café inquiry through distributor handoff and repeat purchasing help grow foodservice sales in the North Bay?

Independent opportunity study based on public sources. This is proposed work; East Bay Projects has not implemented these projects or measured results.

PROSPECTIVE CASE STUDY / SEPTEMBER 2026

THE BUSINESS / PUBLICLY OBSERVABLE

STRAUS FAMILY CREAMERY

Straus reports 170 employees for 2025. Its creamery is a 50,000-square-foot facility in Rohnert Park, with administrative operations in Petaluma. The company makes organic dairy products for retail and foodservice, including a dedicated Barista Milk range. 2025 sustainability report, page 15 ↗

The public wholesale journey is straightforward: discover a product, submit an inquiry, and receive distributor information. Ordering happens through the distributor. What the public site does not tell us is how first and repeat purchases are followed through internally.

THE PROPOSED FIRST PILOT

ONE PRODUCT FAMILY. ONE TERRITORY.

  1. Follow the existing journey.

    Work with Straus sales and one participating distributor serving Sonoma or Marin. Establish what happens to qualified café inquiries today and how first orders are confirmed.

  2. Connect the minimum useful data.

    Link approved inquiry records, distributor acknowledgment, and order evidence. Use existing systems and agreed exports or integrations; verify what data can actually be shared.

  3. Give every handoff an owner.

    Help the team see which accounts need qualification, product evaluation, distributor follow-up, or a next action. Keep the business’s existing commercial process in place.

  4. Measure repeat business.

    Track first and repeat purchases over a window that matches real ordering cadence. Compare an appropriate cohort and include the cost of products, distribution, promotion, and servicing.

HOW THE PAIR WORKS

OPERATIONS EXPERT

Map the sales and distributor workflow, establish the commercial baseline, agree handoff ownership, and check that the team can run the process.

FORWARD-DEPLOYED ENGINEER

Trace the relevant records, connect approved data sources, and build or configure the smallest shared view needed to follow accounts through activation.

PROPOSED MEASURES / NO RESULTS CLAIMED

MEASURE THE WORK.
THEN DECIDE.

Account activation

Qualified café inquiries that become confirmed first orders.

Repeat purchasing

Accounts that reorder, and incremental cases sold over the measurement period.

Contribution & effort

Contribution after costs, alongside follow-up time per account.

WHAT WE NEED TO LEARN FIRST

Can a distributor confirm first and repeat orders? Is product available at a margin that supports new accounts? Are the real constraints coverage, price, product evaluation, or follow-up? A better handoff is a hypothesis to validate.
